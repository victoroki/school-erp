# School ERP — Product Design System

## Register
**Product** — Admin panel for school staff. Design serves the workflow.

## Users
- **Bursar/Finance Officer** — Primary user. Collects fees daily, needs fast access to student records, payment tracking, and receipt generation. Works in office lighting, often multitasking with parents and students.
- **School Administrator** — Oversees operations, reviews dashboards, approves discounts and adjustments.
- **Teachers** — Light usage, mostly student information and attendance.

## Product Purpose
A comprehensive school management system that handles student enrollment, fee management, academics, attendance, and reporting. The fee management module is the most frequently used financial workflow and must feel reliable, clear, and fast.

## Brand
- **Name:** Garikon (visible in logo assets)
- **Context:** Kenyan school (KSh currency, M-Pesa mobile money payments)
- **Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, efficient. No frivolity — this is financial software.

## Color Strategy
**Restrained** — Tinted neutrals with indigo as the primary accent. Amber for financial highlights (fees, balances). Emerald for paid/success states. Rose for overdue/danger. Slate for all neutrals.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Numbers first** — Financial data must be instantly readable. Monospace for amounts, clear hierarchy.
2. **Zero ambiguity** — Payment status is always visible. Balance amounts are always accurate.
3. **Fast workflows** — The bursar processes many payments. Every click saved is meaningful.
4. **Print-ready** — Receipts and statements must look professional when printed.
5. **Consistent surfaces** — All fee-related pages share the same visual language.

## Anti-References
- No AdminLTE default styling (small-boxes, card-primary, etc.)
- No Bootstrap default colors (bg-info, bg-success, etc.)
- No generic "card-outline" borders
- No "btn btn-default" placeholder exports
